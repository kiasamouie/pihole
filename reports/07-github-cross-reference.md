Key intersections:

- **Our deny-standard set vs sources**: every entry is in ≥1 external list or was
  observed on our TV. The 9 `acr-*-prd.samsungcloud.tv` regional endpoints come
  from the SafeNetIoT/ACR research (rank A) — `acr-eu-prd` is our region and is
  the one with the strongest expected hit rate, but it was not observed in our
  six sessions (ACR traffic is event-driven; the TV was used normally, which
  the paper shows is sufficient for ACR to run — its absence in a short window
  is inconclusive, hence all 9 are standard-tier regardless of observation).
- **`acr0.samsungcloudsolution.com`**: present in HaGeZi AND blocklistproject AND
  marq24 AND fightback AND the IMC paper → highest-confidence single deny entry
  in the entire policy.
- **`log-ingestion-eu.samsungacr.com` / `log-config.samsungacr.com`**: OBSERVED ON
  OUR TV (reports/06) and listed by perflyst/blocklistproject respectively.
  Note: `log-ingestion-eu.samsungacr.com` is ALSO blocked by Pi-hole's default
  gravity baseline (resolves to 0.0.0.0 via .128 without any of our rules) —
  the TVGuard layer is the belt-and-braces control, not the first line, for
  that one domain.
- **Ad infrastructure** (`catalyst/rtb/tvx.adgrx.com`, `rtb-config/tvp-ads-log.
  samsungads.com`, `googleads.g.doubleclick.net`, `static.doubleclick.net`,
  `samsungtv-d.openx.net`, `ad/rd.samsungadhub.com`): observed on our TV;
  corroborated by marq24/fightback/hagezi. `2mdn.net` (strict) is a SHARED Google
  ad CDN — deliberately strict-only, low confidence, breakage accepted and
  documented in the policy file.
- **Voice** (`prd-euw1-ucs.aibixby.com`, `provisioning-use2.mgmt.aibixby.com`):
  observed on our TV; no real voice session was exercised, so classification is
  "voice-infrastructure-related", confidence medium, strict tier only.
- **Telemetry** (`qoe.samsungcloud.tv`, `nrdp.logs.netflix.com`,
  `eligibility-panelresearch.googlevideo.com`): observed; `qoe` (Quality of
  Experience diagnostics) and the Netflix/Google research hosts are non-essential
  per purpose + list agreement. The lower-confidence Samsung diagnostic endpoints
  (`devicelog`, `prderrordumphsm`, `notice*`, `oempprd`, `fkp`, `vdterms`,
  `prov`, `sas`, `samsungdm.com`, `nmetrics/smetrics/event-tracking`) are
  standard when 2+ lists agree, strict when single-source.

## 4. Decisions taken from this cross-reference

1. **Exact domains only.** No `*.samsung.com`, no `*.samsungqbe.com`, no
   `*.samsungcloudsolution.*`, no `*.cloudfront.net`, no `*.amazonaws.com`.
   The family-unsafe list in known-breakage.yaml is the enforcement of this.
2. **HaGeZi `native.samsung` was NOT subscribed into Pi-hole.** Its 200 parent
   domains were parsed and cross-referenced instead (hagezi-samsung-analysis.csv).
   Subscribing it wholesale would have wildcarded the samsungqbe/OSP/Smart-Hub
   family and broken the TV.
3. **Perflyst was NOT subscribed** (stale 2023-07); only its breakage annotations
   were used, as evidence.
4. **`unknown-review` (195 rows) is not blocked.** Being "in a blocklist" is not
   evidence of breakage-freedom on THIS TV. They remain candidates for future
   per-domain review once more observation data exists.
5. **The 5 `deny-strict` entries** (`nmetrics.samsung.com`,
   `smetrics.samsung.com`, `event-tracking.samsung.com`, the two aibixby hosts)
   are the difference between standard and strict.

## 5. Residual gaps

- Samsung's own current privacy/ACR web pages are JS-heavy; direct scraping was
  limited. Claims marked CONFIRMED BY SAMSUNG in reports/08 were corroborated via
  their privacy materials and secondary references, not a verbatim scrape.
- `samsungqbe.com` breakage evidence (Perflyst #82) is community-reported, not
  independently reproduced. Treated as real (cost of being wrong = broken TV;
  cost of being kept = one tracker family still active).
- ACR regional endpoints other than EU/UK were not exercised by our TV; they are
  included because the IMC paper shows the same endpoint family per region and
  the TV's region could change (travel, firmware).
